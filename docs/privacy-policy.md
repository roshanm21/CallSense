# CallSense privacy notice

This notice is a development template, not legal advice. Replace the contact
details and retention periods and obtain appropriate legal review before
publishing the app.

## Information used

- **Message scans:** Pasted text is scanned on the device and is not sent to
  CallSense's server by the local red-flag scanner.
- **Screenshots:** Text recognition runs on-device. The selected image is
  processed for text and is not uploaded by the app.
- **Company verification:** The company name is sent to the configured
  Supabase Edge Function and search provider. Public result URLs and a
  registration-date estimate may be returned.
- **Family safety:** Anonymous account identifiers, link records, and alerts
  are stored in the configured Supabase project. Alerts are visible to the
  linked guardian.

## Controls and retention

The project operator controls Supabase and search-provider retention. Configure
retention, access, and deletion procedures before launch. Do not include
passwords, PINs, one-time codes, or other sensitive information in an alert.

## Important limitations

CallSense does not record calls or determine whether a caller is genuine.
Automated text scanning, public search results, and domain-age estimates can be
incomplete or incorrect. Always verify requests through a contact method you
already trust.
