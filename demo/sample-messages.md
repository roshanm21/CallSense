# Sample messages for a manual demo

These are synthetic examples for testing. A scan is an indicator, not proof.

## Likely scam indicators

> URGENT action required: your account will be blocked. Share your one-time
> password now to keep access.

> You have won a prize. Click this link and send money for the processing fee.

## Benign example

> Reminder: our community meeting is at 3 pm on Thursday. Please reply if you
> can attend.

## Discussion prompt

> Your bank will never need you to tell someone your password, PIN, or one-time
> code. Find the official number on your card or the organization's website
> before responding to an unexpected request.
